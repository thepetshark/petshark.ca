# Forest Fellers

*Tim Berman the Forest Feller, an arcade tree-chopping game for iPhone and iPad by Pet Shark Productions Inc.*

Run, chop trees, hop the rocks, collect gems and unlock new Forest Fellers.

## Contact

For help, bug reports or questions, email
[support@petsharkproductions.com](mailto:support@petsharkproductions.com). Please include your device model and iOS
version.

## Your progress

Your best score, gems, unlocked characters and settings are saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes your
progress. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud), the backup includes the save.

## Game Center

Sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app to post your score to the leaderboard and earn achievements. The game
plays fully without it.

## Ads and purchases

Forest Fellers has no ads and no in-app purchases.
