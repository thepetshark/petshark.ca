# Forest Fellers — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 30, 2026*

Forest Fellers (Tim Berman the Forest Feller) is a game by Pet Shark Productions Inc. We made it so that it does not
collect personal data.

## What we collect

Nothing. Forest Fellers has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. It does not track you
across apps or websites.

## What stays on your device

Your best score, your gems, the characters you have unlocked and your settings are saved in a file on your device only.
Deleting the app deletes it. We never see it. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud), the backup may
include this file, under Apple's terms.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your leaderboard scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center service
under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center data. The game works fully without Game Center.

## Ads and purchases

Forest Fellers shows no ads and sells nothing. There are no in-app purchases.

## Children

Forest Fellers is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
