Testing and review 28 and 29 September

Mark-ups, nit-picks and the approval

Two more rounds, judged on the phone. I marked up screenshots with what to change, and the agent ran its own review of the screens as well; the finished version has thinner rows, "Max" at the top of an upgrade, and currency icons beside the prices. My last play-test notes (a laggy slider, the scythe's spin, two sounds) were fixed on the same branch before it was merged.

My part
Marked up screenshots, made the last play-test notes and approved the redesign.
The agents' part
Their own review of the screens, every mark-up applied, and the merge.

What I said at the time

the game is looking and working amazingly. you did a very good job.
