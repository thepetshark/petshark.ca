A fresh pass in three bands

The fresh pass started from a player's session rather than the old screens. It gave the screen three bands: Mr Moody and the time at the top, the forest in the middle, and the wallet above three wooden signs at the bottom. The next round turned the shop into one scrolling page with daily rewards, a piggybank and the offers. I approved both as the direction.
