Testing and review 28 September

Every purchase, start to finish

All nine products were bought in the store's test sandbox on the phone, and restored after a reinstall. The "full pockets" notification arrived when it should. A scripted run from a fresh install played from the intro to the end. With that, all six checks of the definition of done were met, and the plan closed.

My part
Made the sandbox purchases on the phone and confirmed the notification.
The agents' part
The scripted run from a fresh install, and the check of every done criterion.

What I said at the time

Note: when purchasing a non-consumable in the Shop, the product button should remain, disabled, and an owned or purchased banner on it would be good, too. this is a way to show the player what they've purchased (as non-consumables) in the game.
