Design and planning 25 and 26 September

A plan before any code

Before any code, an agent (Claude Fable 5.1) read the whole Unity project, researched current idle games and wrote a design plan. Its definition of done was six checks anyone could run: the round timing, a simulated victory window, buying and restoring every purchase in the sandbox, a notification on the phone, the screens and tests, and 60 frames a second on my iPhone X.

I answered its open questions, from the villain to the currencies, and approved it. It was filed on GitHub as thirteen epics with their tasks, each with acceptance criteria.

What I said at the time

Mr Moody is the boneface asset. no new villain design required.
