IdLings Dev Log

Implementation 27 September

Real purchases, real Game Center

The support and privacy pages went up on petshark.ca, the nine products went into App Store Connect, and a StoreKit 2 plugin loaded all nine on the phone. Game Center got 30 achievements and a leaderboard. The supporter pack lets a buyer put their own name in the credits, and anything bought stays in the Shop, marked as owned.

My part
Created the app record and the nine products in App Store Connect, and set up the sandbox tester.
The agents' part
The StoreKit 2 and Game Center plugins, 30 achievements and a leaderboard, and the supporter's name in the credits.
