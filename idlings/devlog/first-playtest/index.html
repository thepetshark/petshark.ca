Testing and review 26 and 27 September

My first playtest

Then I played it on my phone. Lists wouldn't scroll, the damage numbers were too small to read, and one fairy turned into a wall. Each report became an issue: one action per finger and a scroll that works from anywhere in a list, bigger damage numbers with the fairies' hits shown too, press and hold to keep tapping, and a rebalance.

My part
Played it on the phone and reported what was wrong.
The agents' part
Turned each report into an issue and fixed it.

What I said at the time

scrolling lists and windows do not work. as in, the touch-area for scrolling is being captured by UI objects.

the HP text fliers (e.g. "+1") needs to be scaled up.

fairy 8 has finally become a significant wall.
