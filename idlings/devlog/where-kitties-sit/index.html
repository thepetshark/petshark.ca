Workshop: where does a kitty sit?

The kitties placed on their fairies didn't move with them, so a kitty hung in the air while her fairy bobbed and attacked. The first pilot drew each kitty into her fairy's art; my answer was simpler.

What I said at the time

the kitties who are placed ON their fairy don't move with the fairy. we need a solution to this.

The best position for a cat on a fairy, is sitting on the fairy's head

kitty on head is great!
