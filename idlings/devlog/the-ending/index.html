Workshop 27 to 28 September

An ending worth reaching

Unlike most idle games, IdLings can be finished, and it has a funny ending. That scene had five rounds of its own: drawn loops, timing, poses and a few cartoon effects, each recorded in the game for my notes. Since it's a small reward for finishing the game, no spoilers will be shown here.

My part
Described what I wanted, judged each round, and kept it off the store page.
The agents' part
Drew, timed and recorded five rounds.

What I said at the time

That funny ending should be a reward for anyone who finishes the game, so let's not spoil the ending.
