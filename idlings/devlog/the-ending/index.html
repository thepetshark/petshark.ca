An ending worth reaching

Unlike most idle games, IdLings can be finished, and it has a funny ending. That scene had five rounds of its own: drawn loops, timing, poses and a few cartoon effects, each recorded in the game for my notes. It's the reward for finishing the game, so it stays off these pages.

What I said at the time

That funny ending should be a reward for anyone who finishes the game, so let's not spoil the ending.
