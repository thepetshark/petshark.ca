Workshop 27 and 28 September

Workshop: an attack for every fairy

Most fairies were using whatever animation came with the art pack for their attack, a run cycle in some cases. The workshop compared three options: keep them, a lunge built from each fairy's idle frame, or new attack frames generated from her own sprites. I picked the generated attacks, and twelve fairies got one; Marceline kept her sword.

My part
Asked for real attacks and picked option C.
The agents' part
Reviewed the current animations, drew the options, then the twelve new attacks.

What I said at the time

most are visually unrelated and look weird. (such as running anim for attack).
