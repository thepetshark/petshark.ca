Visual direction 26 September

Art: reused, drawn and generated

The characters, cats and forest are the licensed art from the Unity version. The new pieces (HUD icons, the wooden signs, the app icon) were generated with OpenAI's Codex, using the original sprites as references, then post-processed by script into the game's pixel style and palette. Every sound was synthesized for the project.

What I said at the time

use and send the pack assets as references to the image generation. it's not training.
