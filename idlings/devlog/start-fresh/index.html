Mockups 26 September

Variations, then a fresh start

After my notes the agent drew seven variations: where the time controls go, a Time window, the kitties inside Upgrades, a Forever tab, a market-style shop, a Rewards home. Trying to satisfy every note at once was getting nowhere, so I asked for a fresh start. The kitties inside Upgrades and the Forever tab carried on into it.

My part
Feedback on each variation, then the call to start over.
The agents' part
Seven variations, then the fresh pass.

What I said at the time

the HP ring in the mockups is too fat

forget trying to copy other things or appease all the conflicting input. start fresh. think it through.
