Mockups 28 September

Readability: I cannot read anything

Playing on the iPhone X, I couldn't read the game. Before changing anything, the agent measured the problem on the phone, then mocked up the fixes as before-and-after pairs: bigger type, numbers in a rounded font, the forest zoomed in, and sheets that rise almost to the top of the screen. I also freed the design from the Unity prototype.

My part
Raised the problem and judged the before-and-after pairs on the phone.
The agents' part
Measured the text on the phone and mocked up the fixes.

What I said at the time

everything is too small. even with the "Larger Text" enabled in settings, i cannot read anything on the iPhone X screen.

the game's look and design has evolved from the rough unity prototype - you no longer need to adhere to it.

I like the taller windows.
