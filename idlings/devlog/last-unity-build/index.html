Prologue 2020 to 2021

The last Unity build

Through 2020 the gameboard became a game: thirteen fairies turned to stone up the two trees, a clock of my own design in the corner, the reaper beside it, an Upgrades panel, and kitties waiting to join their fairies. I set it aside unfinished in 2021.

These are from the last Unity build. Some earlier screens used a downloaded placeholder clock, so they are not shown here, and the placeholder mushrooms on the grass are painted out.
