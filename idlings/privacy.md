# IdLings — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 27, 2026*

IdLings is a game by Pet Shark Productions Inc. We made it so that it does not collect personal data.

## What we collect

Nothing. IdLings has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of ours. It does not track you across
apps or websites.

## What stays on your device

Your game progress is saved in a file on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it. We never see it.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details; the game only learns
which item was bought, from Apple, on your device.

## Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your achievements and leaderboard scores are sent to Apple's Game Center service
under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your Game Center data.

## Notifications

If you allow them, reminders (for example, "the fairies have gathered all the Dust they can hold") are scheduled on
your device. Nothing is sent to us.

## Children

IdLings is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.

Questions about this policy: see [IdLings support]({{ "/idlings/" | relative_url }}).
