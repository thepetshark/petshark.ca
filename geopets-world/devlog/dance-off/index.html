Dance-Off capture scene

A playable mockup of catching a Wildling: tap a sighting on the map, choose a dancer, dance, then throw a lasso. Dance controls and progress displays can be switched; the creatures are low-poly placeholders.

Approved as a mockup; building it was deferred until the levelling and evolution rules are redesigned.
