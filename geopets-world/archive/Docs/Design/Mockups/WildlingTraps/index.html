A biscuit.
A little patience.

Craft a wooden trap. Choose a spot on the map. Come back to find out who visited. Wildlings are the creatures that visit the traps.

Walk through the loop

Try the interaction

The trap shortcut is always above Workers. Open it, choose Place trap, then pick a trap, bait and location. Only final confirmation spends your supplies.

Text notifications

Use the small icons on the right to hide workers or traps. Their lists and timers keep working. Rapid presses show the three-row stack.

Each message holds for 1s, then fades for 0.5s. New messages go below older ones. Previous messages still holding become 50% opaque without restarting their timer.

Prototype values

5 Wood → 1 trap / 30s.
1 trap + 1 Biscuit per placement.
60s wait, then 24h to collect.

Both successful and empty traps say “Ready to open”. Uncollected traps break; nobody receives their contents.

Illustrations, Wildling names and the zero-stock trap/bait types are layout placeholders. This is a browser study with sample state, not Firebase or live gameplay.
