A small sale notification while you explore or browse. Open History when you want to see what sold and how much you earned.

WHAT TO TRY
  • A dismissible notice, without a modal or forced navigation.
  • History is one tap away from any market section.
  • Completed sales only, newest first.
  • Quantity, proceeds and time stay visible in each row.
  • Open a row for a read-only sale receipt.
  • No buyer identity or extra reward-claim step.

Example sales are loaded. No notification is replayed for older history.
