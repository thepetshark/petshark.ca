If you can reach only one biome

Big biomes may keep a player in one place for a long time. Nothing is out of reach: goods from other biomes are bought on the market, paid for by selling your own and by request boards. Pick a home to see what its player catches, buys and sells at each tier.

Every home at a glance

Buildings are bought with items, never coins. The house level is the player's level: each level costs a set of items and a number of library stars, and opens the next buildings. Up to house level 3, everything is built from goods found in every biome. Upgrades add a queue slot and speed. Tap any building for its recipe and gates.

Catching a Wildling

  1. Tap a Wildling you can see on the map.
  2. Pick one of your own Wildlings to dance.
  3. If your dancer's level is high enough and its dance is one the wild Wildling loves, the wild one is entranced.
  4. While it is entranced, throw a lasso strong enough for its level.

Every player starts by choosing one of three baby Wildlings, so there is always a dancer. The dance rules below are a proposal.
