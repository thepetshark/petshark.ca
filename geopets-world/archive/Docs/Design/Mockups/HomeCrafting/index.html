GEOPETS / HOME CRAFTING

A little work
at home.

Turn gathered ingredients into something useful. Tap a building, add work, then collect its finished goods.

Placeholder buildings. The Mill and Bakery are original vector studies based on the simple Cottage shape the Home had at the time.

Review a state

State buttons reset this browser study's sample inventory and queues. Visiting World or Inventory does not reset them.

Proposed values and boundaries
  • Mill: 2 wheat → 1 flour, 30 seconds.
  • Bakery: 1 flour + 1 egg + 1 water → 1 biscuit, 60 seconds.
  • 3 slots per station. Ingredients are spent when work is accepted.
  • Completed goods hold their slot until collection. No cancellation in this first slice.
  • No Energy cost, construction, unlocks, real account or backend.

All values and station names are proposals. Timers use one shared mocked engine with saved timestamps during this page visit.
