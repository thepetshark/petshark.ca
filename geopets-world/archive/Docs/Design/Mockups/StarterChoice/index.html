Starter choice

A mockup of a new player's first choice: one of three baby Wildlings, the game's own creatures, picked once, after the save loads and before the map shows. Each starter dances its own style, because dancing is how Wildlings are captured. The three were picked by the designer: Petal Bud, Ember Spark and Tide Shell.

What is proposed here

  • The screen shows once per player, on their first session, over the map as it loads. It cannot be skipped: the player needs a dancer from the start.
  • One starter at a time takes the stage and dances its style. The three round buttons switch between them.
  • The button names the Wildling it will choose, so a mis-tap can't pick the wrong one.
  • After the choice, one screen says it has joined and how to use it, then the map.
  • Each starter's dance, the style it wins over, and where its wild relatives live come from the capture-ladder study.
