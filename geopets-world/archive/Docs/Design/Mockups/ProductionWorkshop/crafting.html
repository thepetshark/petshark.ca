Current game uses unmodified 390 × 844 Unity captures. The 360 × 640 current view scales the same capture for comparison; it is not a native compact capture. The proposed recipe and inventory data are a small illustrative subset: Bakery has 11 recipes in the live capture. No catalog, player inventory, queue, or building data is changed.

In the proposal, arrows and horizontal swipes explore stations. “All crafting” and “Item path” are compact entries within the existing sheet; their placement and behavior remain proposals. Tap a recipe to select it, then use the single fixed bottom action. Current gameplay remains manual and per station.
