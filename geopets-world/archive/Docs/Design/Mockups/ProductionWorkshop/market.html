Market: Buy & Sell

A clickable portrait proposal for navigating listings and composing a sale. Counts, coins, offers and transactions here are fixture values; this page never contacts the game or Firebase.

Try New listing → scroll the full Sell panel and select an item → edit quantity or price → Review → Post. Back from the form returns to the item grid and keeps the draft. Press and hold +/−, press Escape, or tap outside a sheet. Buy an offer to see its SOLD card. In Sell, collect one payment or all pending payments with no confirmation.
