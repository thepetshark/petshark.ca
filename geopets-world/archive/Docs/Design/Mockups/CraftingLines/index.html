Production lines

A concept for Hometown crafting: link crafting buildings into a line, queue what you want at the end, and only finished goods reach your inventory. Buildings and Machines use the live Loom and Ropewalk recipes. Hybrid is the working direction: the fibre family split along the flow of materials, a Trapper that finishes, and rare materials going into parts along the way.

Not a game screen. Each lane is one building; each block is one crafting step, coloured by its queue order. The line works on one order at a time. Within an order, steps wait for their inputs, then take the first free building of their kind.
