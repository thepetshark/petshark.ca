House catalog

A mockup of the gated catalog. Tapping your house opens it: buildings are bought with items, never coins, and gated by your house level, the buildings you own and their levels. Upgrades and levelling up the house live here too. Hometown is the player's own village; the house level is the player's level, raised with items and with library stars earned by catching species. Buildings, costs and gates come from the capture-ladder study. The player's buildings and items are made up, and every number is a placeholder.

The example player

The same player as in the Library view: house level 6, with 21 library stars. They own nine buildings and carry what the Items section of each page shows. Buying really spends their items in the mockup; Reset puts them back.

What is proposed here

  • The catalog opens from your house. The house level is your level (a Town Hall model), so the house is where you grow.
  • Every locked or unaffordable row says exactly why: the house level, the building it needs, or the items it's short of.
  • A missing item offers its route: make it at the building that crafts it, or find it on the market (drops and other biomes' goods included).
  • Levelling up the house asks for items and library stars, with a link to the library.
