A compact four-column grid, based on the original inventory layout. Recognize the item first; inspect its seller and stack details when you tap.

WHAT TO TRY
  • 24 complete offer cards at 390 × 844.
  • Quantity at the corner; total stack price below.
  • Seller details appear on selection.
  • Browse, sell and your listings stay one tap apart.
  • Offline use is blocked; uncertain purchases are checked, not resent.

Every dialog closes when its backdrop is tapped, and buying, posting and withdrawing each ask for confirmation. Clickable design mockup only. No backend calls, real trades or saved player data. Assortment, balances, recipes and prices are illustrative. The item icons come from a purchased UI icon pack and are shown at reduced size.
