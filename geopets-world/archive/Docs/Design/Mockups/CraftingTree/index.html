AN OPTIONAL POOL OF IDEAS

World materials

A small practical core uses workers. The wider assortment is actively harvested from plants, fungi and deposits. Fallen or shed drops are in a separate future idea pool. Levels and rarity are proposals; only collectible materials and the next progression tier would appear on the map. Icons will be generated in the existing UI style.

Growing into something new

Verified vendor form families supply the art for an evolution experiment alongside ordinary Quirky animals. Requirements, costs and timing are intentionally undecided. These forms are not registered in runtime content.
