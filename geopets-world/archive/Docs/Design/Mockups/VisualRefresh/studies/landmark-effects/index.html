The landmark effects: Orb post, Spring and Pavilion

The first effects for these landmarks. The effects tell the state: the orb glows when Energy is ready and dims while it cools down; the Spring runs while Water is ready and goes dry while it cools down; at night the orb lights itself, the Spring's water keeps a faint glow, and the Pavilion has a warm lantern. Here they play on the map at the three zooms, by day and at night, with two or three options each.

Chosen on 22 September: the Orb post's A, with C's ring pulsing out when it is ready and when Energy is collected; the Spring's A; the Pavilion's A. The page opens on that choice; the other options stay for comparison.

These are the real models, drawn in 3D in the browser through the game's own camera numbers, over map plates of 20 September with today's interface on top. Every effect uses what Unity would use: an emissive material, a water shader, a scrolling band, a vertex wave, a few sprites and particles, and at night a light added to the ground in its own colours. It is still a browser, not Unity. The cooldown here lasts 6 seconds so that you can see both states; in the game it is 100.
