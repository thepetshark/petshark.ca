Terrain references: shores, rocky Stonefield and roads

These references support work on the generated terrain meshes, so a result can be compared against them. They follow four earlier images that were crops of one painted world, seen from angles the game never uses; these are more of that world, seen the way the game sees it: ten are the game's own first-trial screenshots redrawn as they could look, so a result can be laid beside its target, and seven are close studies of one feature each.

Generated concepts. They are targets for the look and the proportions. The generator keeps a screenshot's camera and layout closely but not exactly, so no target is a measurement of where a shore or a road is: the geometry stays the game's. The images follow these decisions: roads are drawn onto the rock and nothing is carved for them, only big boulders keep clear of a road; a small pond sits in a slight hollow; and Stonefield should read as rocky mountains whose height has been muted, not as a flat plateau.
