Chosen for the game: the ring and the marker as Settings options

Three rings and three markers from the study became choices in Settings: Soft Edge, Marching Dashes and Pulse, and Ground Compass, Waypoint Gem and Hometown Tether. This page mocks that Settings screen. Tap an option on the Settings screen and the three phones beside it change, live, at the game's three zooms.

A browser mockup through the game's own camera numbers, not a Unity capture. The Settings screen is drawn in the game's own colours and layout; the Pulse ring and the Waypoint gem are the proposed defaults.
