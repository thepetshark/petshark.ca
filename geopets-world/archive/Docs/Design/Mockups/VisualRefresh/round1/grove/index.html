The current look, pushed

The comparison baseline. Everything here is what the game already uses — its cream and green, Nunito, the approved outlined symbols, the selected app image, the real splash painting, a real map capture, the approved Home concept and the real creatures — with a design system added on top. Nothing on this board was generated.

Concept only. Wildling names, rarities, biomes and item titles are real; levels, counts and prices are illustrative. One symbol, the wooden trap, was drawn for this study in the existing family's style; it is not one of the approved symbols.

What was added to the current look

Depth in three stepsA chip, a card and a sheet now sit at three different heights, with shadows tinted by the ink rather than grey, so the screen says what matters most.
Colour that carries meaningThe tint behind a Wildling is its biome. The ring around it is its rarity: plain, water blue, or honey gold. Item wells are tinted by kind.
A title where WORLD has weightThree treatments set in the identity's own two typefaces, one of them using the approved globe symbol as the letter O.
A night modeThe same components on dark surfaces, so the interface stops glaring against the night map. Symbols keep their cream backing, as the identity already specifies for dark terrain.

Three code-native titles, set in Nunito and Fraunces with no generated art. The app icon is the game's selected companion image; it is shown unchanged.
