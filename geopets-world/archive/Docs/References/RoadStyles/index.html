Real-road dirt-path comparison

Actual Unity Game-view captures, 20 September 2026. Every image is 390×844, taken at noon with the same camera at each place. Each row is one place; the columns differ only in which roads are drawn and how they look. Click an image for its original pixels.

The worn-paths and main-roads views share their dirt material and width settings, so only the road selection differs. The all-roads view also uses a wider, paler treatment. The main-roads view preserves real bends and junctions; it deliberately removes most park-only trails.

Central Park has white shoreline rectangles in all modes. Their cause was not yet known when these were captured. The comparison verifies road presentation, not the entire terrain renderer.
