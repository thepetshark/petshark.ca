**Geo Pets World** is a portrait, location-based creature-collecting game for Android and a rebuild of my original Geo Pets. Open the map, discover and collect what is nearby; workers, crafting, traps and a player market, and a floating Hometown, support that.

## Dev Log

The [Geo Pets World Dev Log](/geopets-world/devlog/) shows the design experiments from building the game, in the order they were made: working browser mockups for the map, the interface, Hometown, crafting and the creatures, including many ideas that were explored and set aside. AI agents made the mockups under my direction.
