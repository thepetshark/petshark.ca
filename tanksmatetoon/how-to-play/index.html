Tanks Mate 2: Toon Tanks

How to play

Tanks Mate 2: Toon Tanks is a tank battle for one to four people on the same home network, with bots to fill the other seats. This page shows how to get it running, what everything on the main menu and in the lobby does, and how to drive and shoot.

Download for Windows Version 0.2.30, a 105 MB zip. Free to download and play.

Get it running

  1. Download the zip and unzip it anywhere. Keep the folder together.
  2. Open the TanksMateToon folder and run TanksMateToon.exe.
  3. Windows may say Windows protected your PC, because the game is not code-signed. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  4. On the PC that will host, Windows Firewall asks about the game the first time it runs. Click Allow. The box can open behind the game, so press Alt + Tab to find it. If that PC's network is set to Public, tick Public networks as well.
  5. Put the same version on every PC that will play together. The version is at the bottom right of the main menu.

The game needs Windows 10 or 11. It plays over a local network only, so everyone has to be on the same Wi-Fi or wired network. One PC is enough to play: QUICK MATCH starts a game against bots.

The main menu

The main menu is where you name yourself and either start a game or join one. The tank on the pedestal is only for show. You pick yours in the lobby.

  1. 1

    YOUR NAME

    The name the other players see, up to 14 characters. The game makes one up the first time, such as Tanker90, and remembers what you type.

  2. 2

    HOST A GAME

    Opens a lobby on this PC. Friends on the same network see your game in their list and join it. Nothing starts until you press START MATCH in the lobby.

  3. 3

    QUICK MATCH

    Starts a match against bots straight away, with the mode and the number of bots you used last. The first time, that is a free for all against three bots.

  4. 4

    PRACTICE

    The same as Quick Match, except that the bots drive around and never shoot or use their abilities. A good place to learn the controls.

  5. 5

    CONTROLS

    Lists every control for keyboard and mouse and for a gamepad, and holds the options. They are under Controls below.

  6. 6

    QUIT

    Closes the game.

  7. 7

    GAMES ON YOUR NETWORK

    Games hosted on your network show up here a second or two after they start. A row gives the host's name, the mode, how many people are in and the host's address. Click a row to join. A game on another version shows that version and cannot be joined, and a game with four people in it says FULL.

  8. 8

    OR JOIN BY IP ADDRESS

    If a game does not show up in the list, type the host's address here and press JOIN. The host's lobby shows the address to type. This line also shows your own PC's address.

  9. 9

    The version

    Everyone playing together needs the same one.

The lobby

Hosting or joining a game takes you to the lobby. Everyone picks a tank here, and the host sets up the match. The first picture is what the host sees in a free for all. A player who joined sees the same screen without the host's buttons.

  1. 1

    Tank

    SCOUT or BRUISER. The Scout is quicker and reloads sooner. The Bruiser is slower, and each of its shells does more damage. The bars underneath compare them.

  2. 2

    Skin

    26 paint jobs, from Cherry to Penguin. Two people cannot wear the same one.

  3. 3

    Ability

    One of six, with a line underneath saying what it does. They are listed under In a match below.

  4. 4

    Match options

    Only the host can change these. MODE is FREE FOR ALL or TEAMS. BATTLE TIME is 5, 10, 15 or 20 minutes. KILL TARGET is 5, 10, 15 or 25 kills. WEATHER is CLEAR, RAIN, FOG, SNOW or RANDOM, and TIME OF DAY is DAY, DUSK, NIGHT or RANDOM.

  5. 5

    The host's address

    What a friend types under OR JOIN BY IP ADDRESS if the game is not in their list. A player who joined sees Connected to the host here.

  6. 6

    The seats

    Ten seats, for up to four people and the bots. A row shows the name, the tank and the ability. A person's row also shows their bot level and whether they are ready. The other people's tanks stand on the three pedestals above.

  7. 7

    LEAVE

    Back to the main menu. If the host leaves, the game ends for everyone.

  8. 8

    CONTROLS

    The same list of controls and options as on the main menu.

  9. 9

    BOTS

    Host only. Each click takes one bot away, from nine down to none, and then starts again at nine.

  10. 10

    LEVEL

    How good the bots are against you, from VERY EASY through EASY, NORMAL and HARD to VERY HARD. Everyone picks their own, and a bot plays at the level of whoever it is fighting.

  11. 11

    READY

    Press it when you have chosen. Press it again to go back to choosing.

  12. 12

    START MATCH

    Host only. It lights up once every person is ready.

  1. 1

    TEAMS

    Red against Blue, five seats a side. Your shots, mines and rams do not hurt your own team. The kill target counts the whole team's kills, and is 10, 20, 30 or 50.

  2. 2

    TEAMMATES

    SOLID or PASS THROUGH. With pass through, tanks on the same team drive through each other.

  3. 3

    BOTS on each team

    Host only. The arrows set how many bots each team gets, from none to five.

  4. 4

    JOIN BLUE or JOIN RED

    Moves you to the other team.

  5. 5

    HUMANS VS BOTS

    Host only. Puts every person on Red and fills Blue with bots, one more than there are people.

On a gamepad in the lobby, X changes the tank, LB and RB the skin, LT and RT the ability, A presses the highlighted button and Y opens the controls.

The CONTROLS button also holds the options. Aim assist is OFF, NORMAL or EASY, and nudges your aim toward a tank near the crosshair. With Lock-on switched on, the right mouse button or a click of the right stick locks the turret onto a tank. Mouse up / down is INVERTED or NORMAL, and starts out inverted. Display is FULLSCREEN or WINDOW. Emotes from others is ALL, PEOPLE ONLY or NONE.

In a match

A match starts with a three second countdown. In a free for all the first tank to reach the kill target wins, and in a team match the first team. If the clock runs out first, whoever is ahead wins. A destroyed tank comes back after three seconds and cannot be hurt for the first two. After the results, everyone goes back to the lobby.

  1. 1

    Scoreboard

    Every tank's kills and deaths. It stays on screen for the whole match.

  2. 2

    Clock and target

    The time left and the kills needed to win. In rain, fog, snow, dusk or night, the conditions are written underneath.

  3. 3

    Radar

    You are the white arrow. Enemies are red dots, teammates green ones, and power-ups show in their own colours. An enemy that has just fired shows at the rim even when it is out of range.

  4. 4

    Kill feed

    Who destroyed whom, and the emotes people send.

  5. 5

    Arrows at the edge

    Each one points at an enemy that is off screen, and pulses when that enemy fires or hits you.

  6. 6

    Health

    Every tank has 100. The bar turns amber at half and red at a quarter.

  7. 7

    Ability

    Its name and its key. The square fills up again while the ability recharges.

  8. 8

    Crosshair

    The white ring is where you are aiming. The barrel has its own ring, which turns green when the gun has caught up with your aim.

Abilities

Speed Burst
Double speed for a second and a half. Ready again after 8 seconds.
Double Shot
Charges as you fire. After four shots, press the ability key to fire two extra shells.
Shield
Takes one hit for you. It stays up for 8 seconds at most, then needs 25 seconds.
Mine Layer
Drops a mine behind you that arms after a second and does 60 damage. Ready again after 10 seconds.
Homing Missile
Chases a tank within 45 metres, going round buildings on the way, and does 40 damage. Ready again after 12 seconds.
Dash Ram
A short lunge. It does 35 damage to a tank you hit, and nothing can hurt you during it. Ready again after 9 seconds.

Power-ups

Repair
Gives back 40 health at once.
Rapid Fire
Reloading takes less than half as long for 10 seconds.
Triple Shot
Three shells at a time for 10 seconds.
Bouncing Shells
Shells bounce off walls twice for 10 seconds.
Mega Shell
Two and a half times the damage for 10 seconds.

Weather and night

Rain, fog and snow
Cut how far you can see, and how far the radar reaches.
Snow
Also makes the streets icy, so tanks are slow to get going and slow to stop.
Dusk and night
Darker, with the street lamps lit. F or Y switches your headlights. They light the street ahead, and they let other tanks spot you from twice as far.

Emotes

Sending one
Hold V, point the mouse at a face and let go. On a gamepad, hold D-pad down and point with the right stick.
The eight
LOL, Hi!, Nice shot!, Taunt, Woohoo!, Noooo, Sorry! and GG.
The limit
One every two seconds and three in ten.

Driving over a power-up box picks it up, and a new power-up replaces the one you had. The Dev Log tells how each of these was built.
