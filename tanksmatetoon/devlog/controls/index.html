Second test build To my kids 4 October, 15:55

Gamepads, camera views and an aim assist

4 October, 13:46 to 15:52

The next round was about controls. Gamepads work in the menus and in battle, a controls panel shows both layouts, the host picks the battle time, and the bots got funny names of their own. The camera got a close default view behind the turret, a high view on the right mouse button and a scope. An aim assist and a lock-on went in to try out.

The agent also moved its tests of the built game onto a hidden desktop of their own, so they never take over my keyboard and mouse.

In this build

  • Gamepad support, alongside keyboard and mouse.
  • A controls panel showing both, and a reminder of the controls in your first match.
  • A battle-time option for the host, and funny, unique names for the bots.
  • Three camera views, close behind the turret, high above, and a scope.
  • An aim assist and a lock-on, both to try out.
