0.2.8 To my testers 5 October

Two tanks, exploding cars and a radar

5 October, 01:30 to 08:10

The second big batch was built overnight. It added the Bruiser, a slow, heavy tank beside the quick Scout, and a lot of feedback in battle: hit markers, a radar, and a camera that shows you who got you.

In this build

  • The Bruiser, slower and harder-hitting than the Scout.
  • Cars catch fire when they are hit and then explode. Barrels go up at one shot.
  • Hit markers, a kill confirmation, a death camera that finds who got you, and sound placed in 3D.
  • A radar, with arrows pointing to enemies off the screen.
  • Power-ups you can save in three slots, and a Shield that recharges.
  • A kill target for the host, 5, 10, 15 or 25.
  • The town's courtyard and a tank behind the main menu, and a layout that fits any screen shape.
  • Tanks can no longer climb walls, and damaged tanks smoke and spark from the engine deck.

0.2.7: held back

In its test the Bruiser leaned over and rode too high when it turned, because of how the tank's weight was set up. The agent fixed it, and the same batch went out as 0.2.8 half an hour later.
