0.2.3 To my kids 4 October, 20:06

The camera workshop and numbered builds

4 October, 15:59 to 20:00

I wanted the camera and the driving right before anything else was built, so the feature work stopped. The agent put every number behind driving, aiming and the camera into one tuning file that could be changed in the Editor during a match, and made a build after each round of my notes.

From here every build had a version number and a folder of its own, so no build overwrote an older one. 0.2.1 and 0.2.2 were workshop builds, and 0.2.3 was the first numbered build to go to my kids.

In this build

  • The turret aims where you point, and the camera follows the turret.
  • Live tuning of every driving, aiming and camera number in the Editor.
  • Random respawn points away from enemies.
  • Quick Match and Practice on the main menu, and a choice of up to three bots.
  • Black and White tanks.
  • Bots take the last seats, so friends who join get the next ones.
