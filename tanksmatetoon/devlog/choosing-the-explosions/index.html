0.2.6 To my kids 5 October, 01:01

Choosing the explosions

4 October, 20:39 to 5 October, 00:55

I wanted bigger explosions, and I had two effect packs from the Unity Asset Store, Epic Toon FX and Cartoon FX. The agent built a playground scene that showed their explosions, smoke, sparks and splashes beside its own at the game's scale. I picked from those sheets, and the agent put my picks into the game.

Camouflage and cow-print tanks joined the plain colours, and the camera got a gentle up-and-down look, the setting I chose.

In this build

  • The effects I picked in the playground.
  • Floating power-up boxes from Epic Toon FX, with the game's own icons.
  • Forest, Desert and Snow Camo, and the Moo-Moo Cow.
  • Bots pick a free skin at random, and a player can take a skin from a bot.
  • Names stay readable on dark tanks.
  • Shells can no longer slip through a tank.
