0.2.20 To my kids 8 October, 19:06

Fair explosions and free respawn points

8 October, 17:44 to 18:49

Playing 0.2.19 turned up two unfair moments: taking damage from a tank you had just destroyed, and coming back to life on top of another tank. Both were fixed the same evening, and 0.2.20 went to my kids at 19:06.

In this build

  • Your own blasts spare you and your teammates, and a tank that blows up hurts nobody.
  • A tank only comes back at a free respawn point, never one with a tank close by or one just used.
  • 26 respawn points instead of 16, a short wait for a free one, and a moment just after coming back when a tank can pass through other tanks.
