0.2.19 To my testers 8 October, 16:40

Bot tactics by level

8 October, 15:35 to 16:32

The last part of the difficulty plan gave the bots tactics that build up with their level. It went to my testers as 0.2.19, 4 days and 17 hours after the first commit. Nobody has played the hardest bots yet.

In this build

  • From Easy up, bots go for repairs.
  • From Normal up, they stay with their teammates and take cover.
  • From Hard up, they dodge shells and finish off wounded tanks.
  • At Very Hard they wait round the corner where you disappeared. Very Easy bots do none of it.
  • The ability icon shows in full colour when it is ready, and Speed Burst's wind streaks stream out behind the tank.
  • Easy bots miss a little more.
