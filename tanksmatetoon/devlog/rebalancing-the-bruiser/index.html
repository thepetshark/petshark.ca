0.2.17 To my testers 7 October, 04:49

Rebalancing the Bruiser, and easier bots

6 October, 22:00 to 7 October, 02:31

My testers could not win with the Scout against the Bruiser. In the town's tight streets the Scout had no room to use its speed, and in the chaos not every shell hits, so the Bruiser's slow reload hardly mattered. It also had more health and hit harder. I said it could keep any two of those three.

To choose, the agent ran bot-against-bot matches, fired at a human pace, until they gave the same result my testers had. Then it tried the options. The Bruiser kept its heavy shells and slow reload and lost its extra health. The bots also got easier to beat.

In 0.2.17

  • The Bruiser has the Scout's health, 100.
  • Bots see less, notice you later, forget you and miss more. Easy and Normal bots aim where you were a moment ago.
  • The mouse wheel zooms the view, and the middle button turns the sniper view on and off.
