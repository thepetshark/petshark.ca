0.2.17 To my testers 7 October

The logo and twelve new skins

6 October, 16:30 to 21:07

For the logo, OpenAI's Codex drew eight options in different styles. I picked the comic burst with three tanks. Codex cut it out for the main menu and drew the sky-blue game icon from it.

I also wanted tanks with faces and themes, and the two tank models were never made to be repainted. The agent first worked out how their textures sit on them, then wrote a painter of its own. It colours each part of a tank by where it is and projects flat pictures onto it from the front, the top and the sides. Codex drew the pictures, from a shark's teeth to a pirate's skull. Fourteen skins came out of it. I dropped two, sent two back for changes, and gave some of them wheels to match.

In 0.2.17

  • The Tanks Mate 2 logo on the main menu, and the game icon.
  • Twelve new skins, Great White, Timber Wolf, Tiger Stripes, Bumblebee, Jolly Roger, Hot Rod, Galaxy, Sundae, Ribbit, Pepperoni Pizza, Robot and Penguin, for 26 in all.
  • Matching wheels on ten of the skins.
  • The lobby shows the other players' tanks on pedestals again.
