0.2.14 To my kids 6 October, 09:58

Finding a game on the network

6 October, 09:42 to 09:52

On real PCs a game sometimes did not show up in the list. When the searching PC's firewall blocked the host's announcements, it never heard them. Now the searching PC also asks for games, and hosts answer. I tried it again on separate PCs with 0.2.16, and it worked.

In this build

  • A searching PC asks for games, and hosts answer as well as announcing themselves.
  • Hosts still announce themselves the old way too, so 0.2.10 and 0.2.11 can find them.
  • A hint on the main menu about letting the game through the Windows firewall.

0.2.13: held back

Its new hint about the firewall ran across the main menu and over the Quit button. Wrapped, it went out ten minutes later as 0.2.14.
