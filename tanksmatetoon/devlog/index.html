Tanks Mate 2: Toon Tanks Dev Log

Tanks Mate 2: Toon Tanks went from a brief to its 13th test build in under five days, from 3 to 8 October 2026. Four more builds were stopped before they reached my testers, each over a problem the agent found in its own checks.

I wrote the brief, made the design calls, played the builds and passed on what my testers said. Claude Code, an AI coding agent, wrote the code, the tests and the tools, made the game's own sounds and music in code, tested its own builds, and wrote each decision into the project's GitHub issues for me to check. OpenAI's Codex drew the logo, the game icon and the pictures on the newer skins. Times are Pacific time.

  • 4 days 17 h from the first commit to 0.2.19
  • 13 test builds sent
  • 4 builds held back
  • 165 commits
  • 112 GitHub issues
  • 376 automated tests

  1. 3 October

  2. First build Played by me the next morning The brief and the first build A brief for a toony LAN tank game, two art packs, and by half past four in the morning a whole game.

  3. 4 October

  4. First test build To my testers 4 October The over-the-shoulder camera and the whole town My first playtest changed the camera and the size of the battlefield, and the props learned to react.
  5. Second test build To my testers 4 October Gamepads, camera views and an aim assist Controls for keyboard and gamepad, three camera views, and an aim assist to try out.
  6. 0.2.3 To my testers 4 October The camera workshop and numbered builds Feature work stopped until the camera and the driving felt right, and every build got a version number.
  7. 0.2.6 To my testers 5 October Choosing the explosions A playground scene to compare explosion effects side by side, and camouflage and cow-print tanks.

  8. 5 October

  9. 0.2.8 To my testers 5 October Two tanks, exploding cars and a radar The heavy Bruiser joins the quick Scout, cars explode, and hit markers and a radar show what is going on.0.2.7 held back
  10. 0.2.9 To my testers 5 October Power-up slots switched off Saving power-ups for later worked, and after playing it I asked for the old way back.
  11. 0.2.10 To my testers 6 October Ricochets, the scope and climbing cars Fixes from my notes on 0.2.9, from bouncing shells to a scope that looks down the barrel.

  12. 6 October

  13. 0.2.12 To my testers 6 October Team matches and ten seats Ten seats for up to four people, and a team mode, Red against Blue.0.2.11 held back
  14. 0.2.14 To my testers 6 October Finding a game on the network On real PCs a game sometimes did not show up in the list. Now the searching PC asks, and hosts answer.0.2.13 held back
  15. 0.2.16 To my testers 6 October The game's name The game gets its full name and my studio's splash screen, and the bots stop aiming at rooftops.0.2.15 held back
  16. 0.2.17 To my testers 7 October The logo and twelve new skins A logo drawn by OpenAI's Codex, and a skin painter the agent wrote for tank models that were never meant to be repainted.

  17. 7 October

  18. 0.2.17 To my testers 7 October Rebalancing the Bruiser, and easier bots My testers could not win with the Scout, so the Bruiser lost its extra health.

  19. 8 October

  20. 0.2.18 To my testers 8 October Five bot levels, picked by each player Each person picks their own difficulty, the levels are tuned to measured hit rates, and shells leave scorch marks.
  21. 0.2.19 To my testers 8 October Bot tactics by level The higher their level, the more tactics the bots use, from going for repairs to waiting round corners.
  22. Next build Not built yet What the next build fixes Your own explosions no longer hurt you, and a tank never comes back on top of another.
