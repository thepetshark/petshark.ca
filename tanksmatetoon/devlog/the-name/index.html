0.2.16 To my testers 6 October, 15:09

The game's name

6 October, 11:00 to 15:04

The game got its full name, Tanks Mate 2: Toon Tanks, and the same Pet Shark Productions splash screen as my other Unity games. The project, its folders and the game's file keep the working name, TanksMateToon.

In this build

  • The full name in the game, and the Pet Shark Productions splash screen.
  • Some of the camera's up-and-down look is back, slow to tilt away from level and quick to come back.
  • Bots stop aiming at rooftops, and a bot stranded off the streets is put back at a safe spawn point.

0.2.15: held back

Its firewall hint used the game's name, but Windows Firewall lists the game by its file name, TanksMateToon.exe. With the hint corrected, the batch went out as 0.2.16.
