0.2.12 To my testers 6 October

Team matches and ten seats

6 October, 05:07 to 06:22

Team matches had been written up and held back. On 6 October I let them go ahead, with the agent's recommended answers to the open questions.

In this build

  • Ten seats, still for up to four people, with bots in the seats the host chooses.
  • FREE FOR ALL or TEAMS, Red against Blue, with a team score and no friendly fire.
  • Bots know whose side they are on.
  • Team colours on the outlines, the name tags, the radar and the scoreboard.
  • A team lobby with five seats a side.

0.2.11: held back

Its screenshots showed spare pedestals in the lobby, and Red teammates drawn in red on the radar, where they looked like enemies. The agent fixed both, and the batch went out as 0.2.12 half an hour later.
