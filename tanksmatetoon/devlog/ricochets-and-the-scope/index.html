0.2.10 To my kids 6 October, 04:21

Ricochets, the scope and climbing cars

5 October, 18:09 to 6 October, 04:13

My notes on 0.2.9 were small. Bouncing shells should bounce off buildings, the scope should work on squarer screens, and two Mega Shells from a Scout should finish a Bruiser. I also had the rule that stopped tanks climbing onto things switched off, with its code kept.

In this build

  • Bouncing shells ricochet off buildings, fences and concrete blocks.
  • The scope fits squarer screens and looks straight down the barrel, so its crosshair is where the shell goes.
  • Two Mega Shells from a Scout destroy a Bruiser.
  • Blown-up cars keep smoking, and thrown cars no longer balance on their noses.
  • Tanks may ride up onto cars. The rule against climbing is off, with its code kept.
