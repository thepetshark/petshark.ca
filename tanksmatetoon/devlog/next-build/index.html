What the next build fixes

8 October, evening

More play turned up two unfair moments: taking damage from a tank you had just destroyed, and coming back to life on top of another tank. Both are fixed for the next build.

Ready for the next build

  • Your own blasts spare you and your teammates, and a tank that blows up hurts nobody.
  • A tank only comes back at a free respawn point. There are more of them, a returning tank waits briefly for one, and it can pass through other tanks for a moment.
