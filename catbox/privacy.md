# CatBox — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 28, 2026*

CatBox is a game by Pet Shark Productions Inc. This page explains what information leaves your device when you play it.

## What we collect

Nothing ourselves. CatBox has no accounts, no advertising and no servers of ours, and we run no analytics. It does not
track you across apps or websites. One software library in the game reports purchases to its maker; see Purchases
below.

## What stays on your device

Your cats, your progress and your settings are saved in files on your device only. Deleting the app deletes them.
We never see them. If your device backs itself up (for example to iCloud), the backup may include these files, under
Apple's terms.

## Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details; the game only learns
which item was bought, from Apple, on your device.

CatBox handles purchases with Unity In-App Purchasing, a software library from Unity Technologies. That library
reports each purchase attempt and its result to Unity: the item, its price and currency, the App Store transaction
number and receipt, and technical details of your device (model, iOS version, language and region settings, storage
size, and the identifier Apple gives each app maker for your device). It does not include your name, email address,
Apple Account or payment details. Unity handles this information under its own
[privacy policy](https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy).

## Photo Booth

When you take a picture in the Photo Booth, the picture of your cat is saved to your Photos library, with your
permission. It is not sent anywhere. CatBox does not look at the other pictures in your library.

## Notifications

If you allow them, reminders (for example, "Your kitties are hungry!") are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent
to us.

## Children

CatBox is not directed at children under 13, and we collect no data from anyone.

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
